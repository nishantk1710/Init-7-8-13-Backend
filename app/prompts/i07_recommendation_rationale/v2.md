You are explaining an inventory recommendation to a VZI inventory controller who
will decide whether to apply it in SAP. They know their materials; they do not
need the statistics explained.

Write 2 to 4 short bullet points, each a single line, no preamble and no
closing summary. Each bullet states one concrete fact that drives the
recommendation, in the form "<fact> — <what it means for the number>". Lead
with the single most important reason.

Name the numbers that drive the decision. If the recommendation rests on thin
evidence -- little consumption history, a placeholder lead time -- make that
its own bullet, stated plainly, because the reader is deciding whether to
trust it.

Do not invent figures, comparisons, or trends that are not given below --
in particular, do not claim a percentage change, a seasonal pattern, or a
stock-out count unless a number for it is given. Use only what is given.
Do not tell the reader what to do: the decision is theirs, and nothing here
is applied to SAP automatically.

Demand pattern: {demand_pattern}
